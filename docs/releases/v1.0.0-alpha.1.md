# astromech v1.0.0-alpha.1

The first alpha of astromech 1.0: a voice-first copilot for Elite Dangerous on Windows. It follows v0.1.5 and adds almost everything you can see in the app today. This is an alpha. It reaches you through the in-app updater, with no separate pre-release channel, and a lot of it has been tested against recorded journals and a mock game, not yet in Elite itself. The last part of these notes lists exactly what has not been checked in the game.

**Installing.** The build is unsigned, so Windows SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC" (More info, then Run anyway). It installs per user, with no admin rights. First install: run the setup.exe. Later versions: tray, **Check for updates**, then **Restart to update**. The release's `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums. The [guide](https://github.com/spadaval/astromech/blob/main/docs/guide/index.md) covers every page.

## What's new since v0.1.5

### Voice and callouts

- **A ship's computer voice.** Replies and callouts are spoken by Kokoro v1.0, coloured to sit next to Elite's own COVAS. In Settings, choose the speaker (British or American), the colour (Cockpit, Natural or Harsh) and the speed, and press **Preview**. See [Voice](https://github.com/spadaval/astromech/blob/main/docs/guide/voice.md#spoken-replies).
- **Callouts as you play.** astromech now speaks on its own, only for what the HUD does not show: an arrival briefing (the shopping-list state of the system, your visit history, biology other players found, bodies known to EDSM, your Powerplay state), docking, fuel and route, route waypoints, prospector and scans, signals, High Grade Emissions, discoveries, exobiology sampling, materials at the cap, colonisation shopping, mission reminders, and a session summary (also by voice and at logout). Speech is held back in a fight and while you hold push-to-talk. See [Callouts](https://github.com/spadaval/astromech/blob/main/docs/guide/callouts.md).
- **Switch each callout off.** Settings has a switch and a cooldown for every callout, and the prospector thresholds have their own boxes. See [Settings](https://github.com/spadaval/astromech/blob/main/docs/guide/settings.md#callouts).
- **Voice acts sooner.** Releasing push-to-talk is now handled as soon as its short delay ends, which should save roughly 60 ms (not yet measured on Windows).
- **"Yes, sir." confirms.** "Sir" is now a filler word, so "Yes, sir." answers a "Did you mean" question.
- **Voice tester.** You can see when it is in test mode (push-to-talk tests and does not act), and you can review and remove the phrases astromech has learned from you. See [Voice tester](https://github.com/spadaval/astromech/blob/main/docs/guide/voice-tester.md).
- **Voice setup details.** Speech starts as soon as the voice models finish downloading, and a model counts as installed only after its download finished.
- **Fewer mishearings of everyday words.** <!-- pending: y296 --> "All stop" is recognised more reliably.
- **"Paste" is recognised more reliably.** <!-- pending: 8fey -->
- **"Pace" no longer runs "back".** <!-- pending: 8997 -->
- **A confirmed near-miss is not learned.** <!-- pending: 1c66 --> Saying yes to "Did you mean" no longer teaches astromech a phrase that would press keys later.

### Lookups and pages

- **Ask the galaxy by voice.** The nearest station with a service, module or ship; where to sell or buy a commodity, and the best price anywhere; raw material sites; systems in Boom, Famine or War; Powerplay targets; and colonisation progress, what is left and where to buy it. See [Voice lookups](https://github.com/spadaval/astromech/blob/main/docs/guide/lookups.md).
- **Trade.** Sell, Buy, My cargo and Route modes, with prices from Ardent Insight. See [Trade](https://github.com/spadaval/astromech/blob/main/docs/guide/trade.md).
- **Galaxy.** Services, Outfitting and Shipyard searches, plus the facts of the station you are docked at. See [Galaxy](https://github.com/spadaval/astromech/blob/main/docs/guide/galaxy.md).
- **Ships, Materials, Engineers and On foot.** Your fleet with live build stats (including your engineered modules and the charge time of charged weapons), a build planner with a shopping list, the material grids with cap alerts, the engineer unlock tree, and your Odyssey suits, weapons and inventory with a bartender trade plan. See [Ships](https://github.com/spadaval/astromech/blob/main/docs/guide/ships.md), [Materials](https://github.com/spadaval/astromech/blob/main/docs/guide/materials.md) and [Engineers and on foot](https://github.com/spadaval/astromech/blob/main/docs/guide/engineering.md).
- **Exploration.** The system's bodies as you scan them, including earlier visits, a Biology page with a sampling helper, and your unsold data. See [Exploration](https://github.com/spadaval/astromech/blob/main/docs/guide/exploration.md).
- **Logbook, Session, Mining and Station.** Your journal history in a database on your PC, a session page, the current mining run (tons refined and tons per hour), and the docked station. See [Logbook](https://github.com/spadaval/astromech/blob/main/docs/guide/logbook.md) and [Mining](https://github.com/spadaval/astromech/blob/main/docs/guide/mining.md).
- **Powerplay.** Your standing, the system's state, what earns merits here, and where your power wants work nearby. See [Powerplay](https://github.com/spadaval/astromech/blob/main/docs/guide/powerplay.md).
- **Colonisation.** A tracker for your construction projects, in loads for your ship, and candidate systems near you. See [Colonisation](https://github.com/spadaval/astromech/blob/main/docs/guide/colonisation.md).
- **Quick ways around.** A command palette (Ctrl+K), and links that open your ship in Coriolis or EDSY and the current system or station in Spansh and Inara. See [Using the app](https://github.com/spadaval/astromech/blob/main/docs/guide/using-the-app.md).

### Routes and plotting

- **A route follower.** astromech keeps one route, puts its next waypoint on the clipboard, and says "Next waypoint" when you arrive. It keeps the route across restarts.
- **Go.** Every search result has a Go button that makes the result's system your route.
- **Paste.** Say "paste" or press the paste button to put the next waypoint into the galaxy map's search box and run the search.
- **Spansh plotters.** Neutron and exact routes are plotted on the Route page and followed from there, and you can import a route. You still plot each leg in the galaxy map yourself. See [Routes](https://github.com/spadaval/astromech/blob/main/docs/guide/routes.md).

### Data sharing

- **Share with EDDN and EDSM, if you want to.** EDDN gets the market and journal data other tools use. EDSM keeps your own flight log. Both have a switch, and the Data sharing page shows what was sent and what was not, with the reason. See [Data sharing](https://github.com/spadaval/astromech/blob/main/docs/guide/data-sharing.md), which lists exactly what is sent where.
- **EDSM copes with a bad event.** A batch that fails is split, so one event EDSM chokes on does not hold up the rest.

### Settings and onboarding

- **First-run setup.** Six steps: find Elite, controllers, push-to-talk and kill switch, microphone, calibration and a health check with a fix button for each problem. See [Getting started](https://github.com/spadaval/astromech/blob/main/docs/guide/getting-started.md).
- **Controls.** My controller shows both sticks with per-action detail, and the Health check now covers every action voice presses and warns when V or Enter is bound in the galaxy map. Presets and the preset picker say when a reload failed. See [Controls](https://github.com/spadaval/astromech/blob/main/docs/guide/controls.md).
- **Settings and the tray.** Grouped navigation, a real Settings page, a dark minimal tray, **Check for updates** in the tray, and a Help link on every page that opens its page of the guide. See [Settings](https://github.com/spadaval/astromech/blob/main/docs/guide/settings.md).
- **Safer config.** One bad value in `astromech.toml` resets only that setting. Opening the config no longer writes a sample that quietly sets Scroll Lock as the kill switch.

### Reliability

- **Only live play is spoken.** astromech catches up on the journal quietly at startup, and an old journal rewritten by a backup or sync tool is never spoken. The game's own journal is recognised as live even when its name sorts oddly.
- **Pages keep up on Windows.** Materials and Builds follow the journal while the game has it open, and material counts stop at each grade's cap.
- **Honest pages.** Ships and Materials say "could not read the journal" rather than show made-up sample data, and pages that refresh in the background keep their last data and say the refresh failed.
- **Smaller fixes.** Stored modules and ship locations stay current between visits, the station clears on Undocked, a Powerplay award the game wrote twice counts once, and the Data sharing page states the limits the sender really uses.

### User guide

- **A page for every feature.** The [guide](https://github.com/spadaval/astromech/blob/main/docs/guide/index.md) now has a page for each part of the app, and the README is current.

## What has not been checked in the game

Everything below needs Elite running. The four sessions are meant to be played in order, on Windows, with the app installed from this release. Each step says what to do and what to look for. Write down a result for each issue ID as you go.

### Session 1: trading, outfitting and engineering

Dock at a station with a market and outfitting, with the app window open. Use a ship you know well.

- **Sell at a suggested station** (astromech-1per). Do: ask "where do I sell" a commodity you carry, fly to the first answer and sell one load. Look for: the price matches the quote, allowing for the age of the data.
- **Buy from an outfitting lookup** (astromech-4su3). Do: ask for a module, then a ship, then a service by voice, and go to the top answer for each. Look for: the station really sells each one.
- **Engineering lookups** (astromech-fipx). Do: ask where to find an engineer or a material for a blueprint you need. Look for: the top answers hold from where you are.
- **Ships page build questions** (astromech-fgv5). Do: for one of your ships, answer the distributor, FSD and shield booster questions from the Ships page alone. Then compare with Coriolis and with the fitted ship in the game. Look for: all three agree within displayed rounding. Also look at a real Loadout with an old (pre-3.0) module and a pre-engineered SCO drive, and note which Modifier labels the game writes.
- **Materials page timing** (astromech-rggm). Do: with the window on Materials, make five changes (collect, discard, trader swap, engineer roll, synthesis). Look for: each shows within 2 s. Then open Engineering, Builds and check that it also follows the game within a second or two.
- **Coriolis and EDSY links** (astromech-ww05). Do: on Builds, with a large engineered ship, click one Coriolis and one EDSY link. Look for: the right build opens in your browser, with the long address and the EDSY `#` part intact.
- **On foot events** (astromech-d0zh). Do: buy, upgrade and sell a suit and a weapon, and save a loadout with mods. Look for: the On foot page shows the right grades and mods, with no `odyssey: unknown` line in the log.
- **Odyssey game data** (astromech-h9ch). Do: compare the weapon upgrade prices the wiki leaves blank, the sale prices of a few data and goods items and the engineer and mod pairs, including Manticore Intimidator grade 2 and Tormentor grade 2. Look for: each value matches the game, or note the correction.
- **Bartender barter rules** (astromech-t71i). Do: at a bartender, try to swap goods and data, swap assets for equal barter value, ask for something large, and overshoot a trade. Look for: whether goods and data can be bartered, whether the rate is equal both ways, and what an overshoot does.
- **Backpack and ShipLocker files** (astromech-t8hg). Do: watch the modified times of `Backpack.json` and `ShipLocker.json` while you do each on-foot action once (pick up, drop, transfer, use, death). Look for: which action rewrites which file.
- **Offer the charge-time fix to Coriolis** (astromech-mf7w). This one needs no game. Do: open a pull request or an issue on EDCD/coriolis about sustained damage above burst for charged weapons. Look for: it is open and linked on the issue.

### Session 2: mining and callouts

Mine in a ring with a prospector limpet launcher and refinery, with callouts at their defaults.

- **Mining with the reaction callouts** (astromech-6920). Do: mine for 20 minutes or more, firing prospectors. Look for: the prospector and scan callouts fire correctly, repeat prospects do not repeat, cooldowns hold, and the Mining page shows tons refined and tons per hour that match your cargo.
- **Arrival, docking and route callouts** (astromech-505u). Do: play a session of jumps, a supercruise arrival, a docking and a followed route. Look for: each callout fires when it should, nothing the HUD already shows is spoken, and speech is quiet in a fight or while docking.
- **Callout notifications on Windows** (astromech-g80r). Do: trigger a callout that also shows a notification, once normally, once with PowerShell blocked, and once with Elite fullscreen and Do Not Disturb on. Look for: the notification appears; the log says "notification failed (PowerShell …)" when it cannot; and whether Do Not Disturb shows, holds or loses it.

### Session 3: exploration, Powerplay and colonisation

Explore for a while, then work for your power, then deliver to a construction site. Turn on Upload to EDSM and Send to EDDN first.

- **Unsold data estimate** (astromech-wta1). Do: after an exploration trip, note the Exploration page's unsold estimate for each system, sell the data, and compare with the sale's `BaseValue` plus `Bonus` in the journal. Look for: within a few percent for each system, with no double count for the arrival star or nav beacon scans.
- **Powerplay standing and targets** (astromech-w1q2). Do: open the Powerplay page, jump to a few systems, and ask for nearby targets. Look for: standing, the arrival state and the targets match the game for your power. Also compare the week total (assignments make a small gap normal), rank rewards, and what happens to rank and merits if you defect.
- **Powerplay collect and deliver rules** (astromech-66pq). Do: check in the game that acquisition supply comes only from Fortified systems within 20 ly or Strongholds within 30 ly, that reinforcement and undermining supply have no distance limit, and that undermining data can be handed in at any system your power controls. Look for: each matches, or note the correction.
- **Uncertain Powerplay activity rules** (astromech-c1sq). Do: go through the activities marked uncertain: conflict progress, where mined goods must come from, restore and reactivate missions, flooding markets, exobiology hand-in, salvage, stronghold carriers, escape pods and capitals. Look for: each rule is confirmed or needs a fix.
- **Colonisation delivery** (astromech-xf8j). Do: dock at a construction site, load a ship, and deliver. Look for: the tracker's progress and load counts match the game after each delivery.
- **EDSM upload** (astromech-shi5). Do: with your commander name and API key set, play a short session. Look for: the Data sharing page shows batches sent and no last error, and the jumps appear in your EDSM flight log.
- **EDDN send** (astromech-uxyq). Do: first run a session with `[eddn] test = true` in `astromech.toml` (the Data sharing page says "On (test schemas)"), then a live session with it off. Look for: no rejections. Also note whether market, shipyard, navroute and fleet carrier materials file times equal their events', and whether journal times and file names use the PC clock in UTC.
- **Logbook import timing** (astromech-tqdh). Do: with a clean data folder, start astromech with your real journal folder and time the first import. Look for: the time it takes. Note any second commander or old Legacy journals, and whether a taxi or dropship adds a ship to the history.
- **Ask Inara's author to whitelist astromech** (astromech-3zoz). This one needs no game. Do: write to Inara's author. Look for: a reply, approved or refused, recorded on the issue.

### Session 4: VR, voice and controls

Do this in the headset, with the real microphone. Set push-to-talk to each of the devices below in turn.

- **Fresh install** (astromech-we0f). Do: on a clean Windows account or machine, install from this release and time yourself from the setup.exe to a working "gear down". Look for: under 5 minutes.
- **Voice tester with real push-to-talk** (astromech-1b43). Do: open Settings, Voice tester, hold push-to-talk and say "gear down". Look for: the transcript, the `landing_gear.down` intent and its key within a couple of seconds, and the gear does not move. Click **Run live**, then switch to Elite within 5 s (the gear lowers), and once stay in astromech (nothing is pressed). Leave the page, or minimise the window, and check that push-to-talk works again within 3 s.
- **Push-to-talk on every device** (part of the real-microphone checks, no issue of its own). Set push-to-talk on mouse 4, then mouse 5, then a button on your HOTAS, then a gamepad button, and use the Key tester first to see each press arrive. Say "gear down" with each. Look for: it works from each device, in the game and with the window closed.
- **Keybind questions** (astromech-8tml). Do: with a button above Joy_32 on a stick that has one, bind it and press it in the game. Look for: whether Elite ignores it, as the Health check says, and note the other open questions in the keybinds doc.
- **Real speech and the VR clip eval** (astromech-cgrw). Do: with `[voice] save_clips = true`, speak your commands in the headset, then run `astromech-core --eval <folder>` on the clips in `data\clips`. Look for: at least 95% execute-or-ask. Also listen for clipped command endings: the audio kept after you release push-to-talk is now `post_roll_ms` (200 ms, down from about 260 ms), so raise it if the last word is cut off.
- **Speaking names** (astromech-ddew). Do: have astromech read system names with spelled sector letters, "D 12 dash 39", Grandidierite, Serendibite, Rhodplumsite, Musgravite, Benitoite, Gleeza and Collinder. Look for: each is read correctly, for example a lone "A" as a letter.
- **Cockpit voice in the headset** (astromech-gl7q). Do: listen with Elite's COVAS in the other ear, and try the Cockpit colour and speed. Look for: it sits in the same register as COVAS.
- **Fly a plotted route and paste** (astromech-rk4s). Do: plot a neutron route and an exact route on the Route page and fly them with the follower. At each leg, open the galaxy map, select the search box, and say "paste" and press the paste button. The paste button must first be set under `[controls]` in `astromech.toml` (see Routes in the guide), and paste is refused on foot. Do this on a flat screen, then in VR. Look for: the next waypoint lands in the search box and the search runs, and the follower moves on at each arrival.
- **Galaxy map keys with the box unfocused** (astromech-9lv6). Do: open the galaxy map with the search box not selected and press V and Enter, and anything bound to the Interface actions on them. Look for: whether the map reacts, so the Health check can warn about it.
- **Plotting macro** (astromech-dq9y). The macro is not in this alpha, so there is nothing to run yet. It stays open for when it is built.
- **WebView2 cost next to Elite** (astromech-7ck3). Do: run `scripts/webview_cost.py` next to Elite on the desktop and in VR, and read the frame time with and without the app window open. Look for: a result within each budget.
- **Overlay spike** (astromech-iw0s). The overlay is not in this alpha. The measuring half of the spike (an hour's stability, GPU and CPU cost, texture sharing, Meta headsets over Steam Link and Virtual Desktop) is still open.
- **Overlay panels in the headset** (astromech-leio). The same applies: there are no panels to check yet.
- **Walk every screen** (astromech-snqt). Do: open every page of the app and note each as ok or rough. On Windows, also click a page's Help link. Look for: the browser opens at the right heading.
- **A week without the old tools** (astromech-y5so). Do: after the sessions, play for a week without opening Inara, Spansh, Coriolis, EDMC, EDSM, EDCoPilot or OD Explorer. Look for: each time you still needed one, and why.

## Known limits

- **Voice.** English only, from a fixed list of commands. Flight commands (supercruise, hyperspace jump, docking requests, route plotting) are recognised but do nothing yet. Speech recognition can still mishear game words: say "Did you mean" answers clearly and use the Voice tester to see what it heard. Whether the voice commands for the left and right panel match what the game writes has not been checked.
- **Daylight saving.** Clocks go back on 25 October in Europe and 1 November in the US. astromech relies on Elite naming its journal files in local time, which was confirmed on one machine only. In the repeated hour, a journal can be judged live or old wrongly, so a callout may be missed or an old one spoken.
- **EDSM is best effort.** astromech sends as much as it can. A batch that fails is split, and an event EDSM keeps refusing is given up after several tries, so it may be missing from your flight log.
- **Callouts.** A stale callout can still fire for a replayed docking in one rare case, when a market file was missing during the replay.
- **Pages.** The Builds page cannot yet find the one station that sells every module a build needs. The Mining page says the core is not running when it runs but no session has started a run. A failed Skip, Back, Clear or Copy on the Route page loses its error after two seconds. The trip planner's engineer list and the header status still hide a failed refresh.
- **Slow start.** The window takes about 2 s longer to open after the core crashed.
- **Unsigned.** The installer is not signed, so SmartScreen warns on each setup.exe. In-app updates are signed and checked.
