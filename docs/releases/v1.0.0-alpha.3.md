# astromech v1.0.0-alpha.3

The third 1.0 alpha. It follows v1.0.0-alpha.2. Neutron routes can now be plotted on your PC, outfitting goes to EDDN, you can ask about the system you are exploring by voice, and a batch of fixes from the alpha reviews. It is also the first build that updates from astromech's new home, [brewer-corp/astromech](https://github.com/brewer-corp/astromech). This is an alpha. It reaches you through the in-app updater, with no separate pre-release channel.

**Installing.** The build is unsigned, so Windows SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC" (More info, then Run anyway). It installs per user, with no admin rights. First install: run the setup.exe. Later versions: tray, **Check for updates**, then **Restart to update**. The release's `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums. The [guide](https://github.com/brewer-corp/astromech/blob/main/docs/guide/index.md) covers every page.

## What's new since v1.0.0-alpha.2

### Updates

- **A new home.** astromech moved from spadaval/astromech to brewer-corp/astromech. alpha.1 and alpha.2 still find this update through GitHub's redirect. From this version on, updates and downloads come straight from brewer-corp/astromech, and every link the app opens points there.

### Routes

- **Neutron routes plotted on your PC.** Download the neutron star file once from the Route page (about 71 MB, every known neutron star). After that, astromech works out a neutron route on your PC in a second or two, with no wait for Spansh. Without the file, Spansh plots it as before. The result says where it was plotted: **on this PC** or **by Spansh**. In our tests against Spansh, counting jumps the same way for both, a route plotted on the PC never had more jumps than Spansh's, and often had fewer. See [Routes](https://github.com/brewer-corp/astromech/blob/main/docs/guide/routes.md#the-neutron-star-file).
- **Six times the range with the Mk II drive.** Both plotters now use your drive's own neutron boost. A Caspian Explorer with the size 8 Mk II supercharge-optimised drive gets ×6 routes instead of ×4 ones.
- **The Route page stays honest.** An error from Skip, Back, Clear or Copy stays until your next action. The ship and range line keeps up when you swap ships or your cargo changes, and the result says the jump range the route was plotted at.
- **What to do at a waypoint, in its own sentence.** "Next waypoint" by voice says the system name on its own, then what to do there: *"Next waypoint. Hyades Sector DB-W b2-6. For fuel."* or *"Next waypoint. Kuk. For Ram Tah. 2 waypoints left."*

### Voice

- **Ask about the system you are exploring.** "What is this system worth", "is this system worth mapping", "which organics haven't I scanned here" and "how much is my unsold data worth". They answer from the Exploration pages and never press a key. See [Voice commands](https://github.com/brewer-corp/astromech/blob/main/docs/guide/voice-commands.md#exploration).
- **Names read as written.** A body such as "LHS 3447 B" is read with a letter B, not "billion", and an M in the middle of a name is a letter, not "million". Jadeite, Taaffeite, Uraninite and Pyrophyllite are said more clearly.
- **Fewer mishearings.** "Notoriety", "frameshift" and "engage" are heard more reliably, and "fuel state" asks for your fuel.
- **Left and right panels.** astromech read the left and right panels the wrong way round from the game's status file, so "left panel" could say "did not respond" after it opened, or "already open" when the right panel was. That is corrected, following Frontier's journal manual. It has not been checked in the game yet.
- **Speech after a crash.** If the speech engine stops, a waiting reply no longer leaves astromech unable to speak.

### Data sharing

- **Outfitting goes to EDDN.** With Send to EDDN on, the modules a station sells are sent when you open outfitting. Modules only you can buy (Powerplay, tech broker) are left out, as EDDN asks. If the game lists a module astromech doesn't know yet, that visit's outfitting isn't sent, and Diagnostics says so. See [Data sharing](https://github.com/brewer-corp/astromech/blob/main/docs/guide/data-sharing.md#eddn-exactly-what-is-sent).
- **EDSM copes with an outage.** Events EDSM never refused go first, so a few bad events can't hold up the rest. During an outage astromech asks EDSM at most three times a minute, and gives up fewer events.

### Journal and callouts

- **Only live play counts, more surely.** A journal that was already there at startup is history, up to where it ended; only lines written after that are live. That holds for speech, uploads and the route. A replayed market file waiting for its data stays silent.
- **Fresher background answers.** A callout's background lookup is retried at a steady pace, and an answer is kept for an hour. EDSM's body counts for a system are refreshed after an hour, not a week.

### Pages

- **Ships.** Each ship you owned keeps its own row, even when the game reuses a traded-in ship's ID. A redeemed ship now has the date you got it. In the build planner, a target at a module's own grade and fully rolled keeps the values from your journal.
- **Logbook.** Journals from before the game wrote a commander ID join your commander of the same name. Search finds names with accents whatever their case.
- **Powerplay.** The page keeps up without re-reading your whole journal every few seconds. Logging in with no pledge clears an old pledge, and a system with a state but no controlling power is named by its state.
- **Mining.** A run starts only with a prospector or collector limpet, not a repair or fuel limpet. Before any mining the page says "No mining run yet." If it can't read the run it says "Could not read the mining run".
- **No silent failures.** The engineer trip, Settings and setup say "Could not refresh" when a refresh fails, instead of showing old data as current. Trade Route says when it couldn't read your ship and credits, instead of assuming 20 ly.
- **Settings by keyboard.** Settings controls show a focus ring, errors and "Saved" are read out by screen readers, and a value astromech refuses is put back.
- **Narrow windows.** The header wraps to a second row once, cleanly, as the window narrows. The Powerplay page has no gaps at any width.

### The app window

- **Faster start after a crash.** The window used to open about 2 seconds late after the core crashed. It now opens as quickly as usual.
- **A missing config file.** If the config file the window was told to use is gone, the window uses the defaults instead of failing.

## What has not been checked in the game

Everything below needs Elite running. These are alpha.1's four sessions, with what is new in this alpha added. Play them in order, on Windows, with the app installed from this release. Each step says what to do and what to look for. Write down a result for each issue ID as you go.

### Session 1: trading, outfitting and engineering

Dock at a station with a market and outfitting, with the app window open. Use a ship you know well.

- **Sell at a suggested station** (astromech-1per). Do: from the Commodities page, ask "where do I sell" a commodity you carry, fly to the first answer and sell one load. Look for: the price matches the quote, allowing for the age of the data.
- **Buy from an outfitting lookup** (astromech-4su3). Do: ask for a module, then a ship, then a service, by voice and from Galaxy Find, and go to the top answer for each. Look for: the station really sells each one.
- **Engineering lookups** (astromech-fipx). Do: ask where to find an engineer or a material for a blueprint you need. Look for: the top answers hold from where you are.
- **Ships page build questions** (astromech-fgv5). Do: for one of your ships, answer the distributor, FSD and shield booster questions from the Ships page alone. Then compare with Coriolis and with the fitted ship in the game. Look for: all three agree within displayed rounding. In the build planner, set a target at a module's own grade, fully rolled, and check its values don't change. Also look at a real Loadout with an old (pre-3.0) module and a pre-engineered SCO drive, and note which Modifier labels the game writes.
- **Materials page timing** (astromech-rggm). Do: with the window on Materials, make five changes (collect, discard, trader swap, engineer roll, synthesis). Look for: each shows within 2 s. Then open Engineering, Builds and check that it also follows the game within a second or two.
- **Coriolis and EDSY links** (astromech-ww05). Do: on Builds and on a ship's Info page, with a large engineered ship, click one Coriolis and one EDSY link. Look for: the right build opens in your browser, with the long address and the EDSY `#` part intact.
- **On foot events** (astromech-d0zh). Do: buy, upgrade and sell a suit and a weapon, and save a loadout with mods. Look for: the On foot page shows the right grades and mods, with no `odyssey: unknown` line on stderr (the release app has no console, so use a debug build or `cargo tauri dev`).
- **Odyssey game data** (astromech-h9ch). Do: compare the weapon upgrade prices the wiki leaves blank, the sale prices of a few data and goods items and the engineer and mod pairs, including Manticore Intimidator grade 2 and Tormentor grade 2. Look for: each value matches the game, or note the correction.
- **Bartender barter rules** (astromech-t71i). Do: at a bartender, try to swap goods and data, swap assets for equal barter value, ask for something large, and overshoot a trade. Look for: whether goods and data can be bartered, whether the rate is equal both ways, and what an overshoot does.
- **Backpack and ShipLocker files** (astromech-t8hg). Do: watch the modified times of `Backpack.json` and `ShipLocker.json` while you do each on-foot action once (pick up, drop, transfer, use, death). Look for: which action rewrites which file.
- **Offer the charge-time fix to Coriolis** (astromech-mf7w). This one needs no game. Do: open a pull request or an issue on EDCD/coriolis about sustained damage above burst for charged weapons. Look for: it is open and linked on the issue.

### Session 2: mining and callouts

Mine in a ring with a prospector limpet launcher and refinery, with callouts at their defaults.

- **Mining with the reaction callouts** (astromech-6920). Do: mine for 20 minutes or more, firing prospectors. Before that, launch a repair or fuel limpet. Look for: the other limpet does not start a run, the prospector and scan callouts fire correctly, repeat prospects do not repeat, cooldowns hold, and the Mining page shows tons refined and tons per hour that match your cargo.
- **Arrival, docking and route callouts** (astromech-505u). Do: play a session of jumps, a supercruise arrival, a docking and a followed route. Start astromech once while Elite is already running. Look for: each callout fires when it should, nothing the HUD already shows is spoken, speech is quiet in a fight or while docking, and nothing from before the start is spoken.
- **Callout notifications on Windows** (astromech-g80r). Do: trigger a callout that also shows a notification, once normally, once with PowerShell blocked, and once with Elite fullscreen and Do Not Disturb on. Look for: the notification appears; the log says "notification failed (PowerShell …)" when it cannot; and whether Do Not Disturb shows, holds or loses it.

### Session 3: exploration, Powerplay and colonisation

Explore for a while, then work for your power, then deliver to a construction site. Turn on Upload to EDSM and Send to EDDN first.

- **Unsold data and exploration questions** (astromech-wta1). Do: in a system you are scanning, ask "what is this system worth", "is this system worth mapping" and "which organics haven't I scanned here". After the trip, ask "how much is my unsold data worth", note the Exploration page's unsold estimate for each system, sell the data, and compare with the sale's `BaseValue` plus `Bonus` in the journal. Look for: the answers match the pages and the game, and the estimate is within a few percent for each system, with no double count for the arrival star or nav beacon scans.
- **Powerplay standing and targets** (astromech-w1q2). Do: open the Powerplay page, jump to a few systems, and ask for nearby targets. Relog from the main menu once. Look for: standing, the arrival state and the targets match the game for your power, and the pledge survives the relog. Also compare the week total (assignments make a small gap normal), rank rewards, and what happens to rank and merits if you defect.
- **Powerplay collect and deliver rules** (astromech-66pq). Do: check in the game that acquisition supply comes only from Fortified systems within 20 ly or Strongholds within 30 ly, that reinforcement and undermining supply have no distance limit, and that undermining data can be handed in at any system your power controls. Look for: each matches, or note the correction.
- **Uncertain Powerplay activity rules** (astromech-c1sq). Do: go through the activities marked uncertain: conflict progress, where mined goods must come from, restore and reactivate missions, flooding markets, exobiology hand-in, salvage, stronghold carriers, escape pods and capitals. Look for: each rule is confirmed or needs a fix.
- **Colonisation delivery** (astromech-xf8j). Do: dock at a construction site, load a ship, and deliver. Look for: the tracker's progress and load counts match the game after each delivery.
- **EDSM upload** (astromech-shi5). Do: with your commander name and API key set, play a short session. Look for: the Data sharing page shows batches sent and no last error, and the jumps appear in your EDSM flight log.
- **EDDN send** (astromech-uxyq). Do: first run a session with `[eddn] test = true` in `astromech.toml` (the Data sharing page says "On (test schemas)"), then a live session with it off. Open outfitting at a few stations, including one with Powerplay or tech-broker modules you have unlocked. Look for: no rejections, outfitting sent, and the modules only you can buy left out. Also note whether market, outfitting, shipyard, navroute and fleet carrier materials file times equal their events', and whether journal times and file names use the PC clock in UTC.
- **Logbook import timing** (astromech-tqdh). Do: with a clean data folder, start astromech with your real journal folder and time the first import. Look for: the time it takes. Note any second commander or old Legacy journals, whether journals from before the commander ID join your commander, and whether a taxi or dropship adds a ship to the history.
- **Ask Inara's author to whitelist astromech** (astromech-3zoz). This one needs no game. Do: write to Inara's author. Look for: a reply, approved or refused, recorded on the issue.

### Session 4: VR, voice and controls

Do this in the headset, with the real microphone. Set push-to-talk to each of the devices below in turn.

- **Fresh install** (astromech-we0f). Do: on a clean Windows account or machine, install from this release and time yourself from the setup.exe to a working "gear down". Look for: under 5 minutes.
- **Voice tester with real push-to-talk** (astromech-1b43). Do: open Settings, Voice tester, hold push-to-talk and say "gear down". Look for: the transcript, the `landing_gear.down` intent and its key within a couple of seconds, and the gear does not move. Click **Run live**, then switch to Elite within 5 s (the gear lowers), and once stay in astromech (nothing is pressed). Leave the page, or minimise the window, and check that push-to-talk works again within 3 s.
- **Push-to-talk on every device** (part of the real-microphone checks, no issue of its own). Set push-to-talk on mouse 4, then mouse 5, then a button on your HOTAS, then a gamepad button, and use the Key tester first to see each press arrive. Say "gear down" with each. Look for: it works from each device, in the game and with the window closed.
- **Left and right panels** (no issue of its own). Do: say "left panel", then "right panel", then each again while it is open. Look for: "Left panel open." and "Right panel open." when each opens, never "did not respond", and "already open" only for the panel that is open.
- **Keybind questions** (astromech-8tml). Do: with a button above Joy_32 on a stick that has one, bind it and press it in the game. Look for: whether Elite ignores it, as the Health check says, and note the other open questions in the keybinds doc.
- **Real speech and the VR clip eval** (astromech-cgrw). Do: with `[voice] save_clips = true`, speak your commands in the headset, then run `astromech-core --eval <folder>` on the clips in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\astromech\data\clips`. Look for: at least 95% execute-or-ask. Also listen for clipped command endings: the audio kept after you release push-to-talk is `post_roll_ms` (200 ms), so raise it if the last word is cut off.
- **Phrases the synthetic eval still misses** (astromech-xi2i). Do: in the same clip session, say "rebuy", "handbrake off" and "drive assist on" a few times each, once with cockpit noise. Look for: whether each is heard right, or what it is heard as, so it can get an alias.
- **Speaking names** (astromech-ddew). Do: have astromech read system names with spelled sector letters, "D 12 dash 39", "LHS 3447 B", Grandidierite, Serendibite, Rhodplumsite, Musgravite, Benitoite, Jadeite, Taaffeite, Uraninite, Pyrophyllite, Gleeza and Collinder. Look for: each is read correctly, for example a lone "A" or "B" as a letter.
- **Cockpit voice in the headset** (astromech-gl7q). Do: listen with Elite's COVAS in the other ear, and try the Cockpit colour and speed. Look for: it sits in the same register as COVAS.
- **Fly a plotted route and paste** (astromech-rk4s). Do: download the neutron star file on the Route page. Plot a neutron route (it should say "on this PC") and an exact route, and fly them with the follower. Say "next waypoint" at a refuel stop. At each leg, open the galaxy map, select the search box, and say "paste" and press the paste button. The paste button must first be set under `[controls]` in `astromech.toml` (see Routes in the guide), and paste is refused on foot. Do this on a flat screen, then in VR. Look for: the next waypoint lands in the search box and the search runs, the follower moves on at each arrival, and at a refuel stop on the exact route the waypoint answer says "For fuel." (or "For neutron boost and fuel.") after the system name.
- **The game's own neutron routing** (astromech-4ytn). Do: plot three to five routes under 20,000 ly in the galaxy map (inside the bubble, out to Colonia's edge, and one off the plane), with "use jet-cone boost" on and off, then plot the same routes on the Route page. If you have a Caspian Explorer with the Mk II drive, do one in it. Look for: the game's jump counts against astromech's, whether the game plots ×6 boosted jumps for the Caspian, and whether it ever detours to a neutron star off the straight line.
- **Galaxy map keys with the box unfocused** (astromech-9lv6). Do: open the galaxy map with the search box not selected and press V and Enter, and anything bound to the Interface actions on them. Look for: whether the map reacts, so the Health check can warn about it.
- **Plotting macro** (astromech-dq9y). The macro is not in this alpha, so there is nothing to run yet. It stays open for when it is built.
- **WebView2 cost next to Elite** (astromech-7ck3). Do: run `scripts/webview_cost.py` next to Elite on the desktop and in VR, and read the frame time with and without the app window open. Look for: a result within each budget, including the window's start after a core crash.
- **Overlay spike** (astromech-iw0s). The overlay is not in this alpha. The measuring half of the spike (an hour's stability, GPU and CPU cost, texture sharing, Meta headsets over Steam Link and Virtual Desktop) is still open.
- **Overlay panels in the headset** (astromech-leio). The same applies: there are no panels to check yet.
- **Walk every screen** (astromech-snqt). Do: open every page of the app and note each as ok or rough, including the Route page's neutron star file and a narrow window. On Windows, also click a page's Help link. Look for: the browser opens at the right heading, on brewer-corp/astromech.

## Known limits

- **Neutron routes on this PC need the file.** The neutron star file is a one-time download of about 71 MB (184 MB on disk), and uses about 80 MB of memory while the window is open. It is rebuilt by hand now and then, not with each update. Until it is downloaded, Spansh plots neutron routes.
- **Routes plotted on this PC.** They don't plan fuel, and they count the normal jumps between neutron stars from the distance. In sparse space, between the arms and at the galaxy's edge, a leg may take more jumps than the page says, or need a detour. On a long route the search stops at a good route instead of trying every one, so it is near the fewest jumps, and the page says so. There is no Efficiency setting for them. See [Routes](https://github.com/brewer-corp/astromech/blob/main/docs/guide/routes.md#plot-a-route).
- **Spansh's jump count.** A neutron route plotted by Spansh shows its number of legs as its jumps, so it looks shorter than it is: Sol to Colonia says 128, not 166 (astromech-6h7t). Routes plotted on this PC count every jump.
- **Plotting.** No FSD injections, via systems or fuel reserve. You still plot each leg in the galaxy map yourself.
- **Voice.** English only, from a fixed list of commands. Flight commands (supercruise, hyperspace jump, docking requests, route plotting) are recognised but do nothing yet. Speech recognition can still mishear game words: say "Did you mean" answers clearly and use the Voice tester to see what it heard. The left and right panel fix follows Frontier's manual and has not been checked in the game.
- **Outfitting for new modules.** A station that lists a module astromech doesn't know yet sends no outfitting for that visit.
- **EDSM is best effort.** astromech sends as much as it can. An event EDSM keeps refusing is given up after several tries, so it may be missing from your flight log.
- **Daylight saving.** Clocks go back on 25 October in Europe and 1 November in the US. astromech relies on Elite naming its journal files in local time, which was confirmed on one machine only. In the repeated hour, a journal can be judged live or old wrongly, so a callout may be missed or an old one spoken.
- **Pages.** The Builds page cannot find one station that sells every module a build still needs. When a status read fails, the header says "Not running" even if the core is running. Info shows approximate pitch, roll and yaw from the ship catalogue, not live telemetry.
- **Unsigned.** The installer is not signed, so SmartScreen warns on each setup.exe. In-app updates are signed and checked.
