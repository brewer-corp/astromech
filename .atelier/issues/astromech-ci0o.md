## Question

The earlier agreements from the Route redesign thread (6 Nov 2026) carry over: scale with window height, orange #ff7100 with label orange #d97a34 at weight 500, an instant Tip in place of native tooltips, a status bar at the foot, and design work done on the real page in the browser preview.
