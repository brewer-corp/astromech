## Finding 1: Keep the neutron route's actions menu inside the window
Recommendation: fix now

In `app/src/modules/route/Title.svelte:201-205` and its `.tools` layout at line 253, the neutron plotter's two cells leave insufficient space for both help and menu controls. The menu wraps onto a second line at the sidebar's left edge. `Menu.svelte` opens its popover to the left of the trigger (`right: 0; width: max-content`), so most of the menu is outside the window, including the beginnings of Import a route, Open on Spansh and Clear route.

Reproduced with `/?core=ready&route=timeline#/materials`: at 1920 x 1080, click More route actions in the docked sidebar. The menu's bounding rectangle is x = -136, width = 204, right = 68. At 1100 x 700, open the sidebar from the strip and then its menu: x = -143, width = 204, right = 61. The capture at `/tmp/uc7i-neutron-menu.png` confirms the clipped labels. The exact-route case fits and does not show the fault, which is why the committed exact-route captures miss it. Keep the controls positioned so the menu can open within the window, or make its placement account for the available space. Verify both neutron and exact plotters in the frame at both widths.

## Finding 2: Honor explicit sidebar opens when there is no route at dock width
Recommendation: fix now

`app/src/App.svelte:63-66` requires a followed or parked route to render the docked sidebar and always renders the rail when both are absent. `RouteFollow.show()` in `app/src/modules/route/follow.svelte.ts:77-79` only clears `collapsed` at dock width, which cannot satisfy that condition. Consequently an explicit Route command cannot open the sidebar in this state.

Reproduced at 1920 x 1080 with `/?core=ready#/route`: the address becomes `#/controller`, but only the No route rail appears. Choosing Route in Find or plot also leaves the rail unchanged. At 1100 x 700 the same cold link correctly opens the empty sidebar and its plotter over the page. This contradicts the Done when line requiring cold `#/route` links and existing Route entry points to open the sidebar, and the guide's promise of that behavior. Keep the rail as the ordinary empty state, but distinguish an explicit request to show the sidebar so it can reveal the empty plotter at dock width too. Cover a cold link and the Route command with no followed or parked route.
