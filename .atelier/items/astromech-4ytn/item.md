## Goal

Know what the game's own plotter already does, so astromech's neutron plotter is aimed at the routes where it actually wins.

## Done when

- [ ] An evidence receipt, from runs in the game, answers: - With "use jet-cone boost" on, does the galaxy map plot ×6 boosted jumps for a Caspian Explorer with the Mk II drive? - For three to five routes under 20,000 ly (inside the bubble, bubble to Colonia's edge, one off the plane), the game's jump count with jet-cone boost on and off, against Spansh's neutron plotter at efficiency 60 and 100 for the same ship. - Does the game ever detour to a neutron star off the straight line?

## Context

Forum reports (Frontier forums, threads 308674, 451960 and 513295) say jet-cone boost routing behaves like about 99.5% efficiency and is sometimes inconsistent. Not yet measured by us. Sumeet asked to come back to this later (22 Oct 2026).
