## Finding 1: Keep stock and the trip reachable when Colonisation rows become compact
Recommendation: fix now

`app/src/lib/ui/SearchKit.svelte:505` and `:512` remove both the own group and the trip whenever `compact` is true, even when `detail` is absent. Keeping the first two groups' second lines only preserves the trip for lists ranked by jumps. Colonisation's where-to-buy list has `main: Sells` and no qualifier, so its first group's second line is empty; the stock, jumps, light years and report age disappear altogether. Its station name is a non-selectable span, and its remaining titles contain only the station and system names, so no panel or Tip recovers those facts.

Reproduction on the recorded preview: open `/?core=ready#/colonisation` at 1100 x 700, then set Text size to 145 percent. At 100 percent, Efremov Plant has five cells including `Steel 25,553 t, CMM Composite 10,061 t, Ceramic Composites 2,120 t` and `1 jump / 8.6 ly · 36 d old`. At 145 percent it has three cells and only `3 of 4 / Efremov Plant L 4,412 ls / Sirius`, plus the actions. There are no stock, trip or age elements in that row. This violates the explicit requirement that panel-less lists retain report age at any age and that no previously shown fact become unreachable. Preserve access to these groups when there is no detail panel, and cover the recorded Colonisation list at this supported size in a browser test.

## Finding 2: Reserve the type-icon space so station names align
Recommendation: defer

The Report's alignment caveat is reproducible. `app/src/lib/ui/SearchKit.svelte:485` conditionally renders the station kind without reserving its space, and `:591-592` removes the system line's indent when the icon is missing. On Sell anywhere at 1100 x 700 and 100 percent, FCX-800's station and system begin at x=217, while SavorgnandeBrazza Legacy's and Asgaard's begin at x=193. Both lines therefore shift by 24 px between rows in the same group, contrary to DESIGN.md's Aligned Columns Rule. This is a cosmetic inconsistency rather than a loss of facts and can follow separately. Reserve a consistent icon slot and indent within the shared place group instead of letting optional source metadata move the text.
