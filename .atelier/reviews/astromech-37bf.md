Low: the natural short forms miss. 'nearest federation' asks 'Did you mean nearest fuel station?' (0.818) and 'nearest empire' asks 'nearest repairs?' (0.714). Only 'nearest federation/federal/empire/imperial system' and '... faction' reach faction.fed_composites/empire_shielding. Suggest adding 'nearest federation' and 'nearest empire' to the phrases.
