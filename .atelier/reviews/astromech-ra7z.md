R4 low: The wiki's implausible values pass through without being flagged. data/catalog/odyssey.json:1605 gives Manticore Intimidator grade 2 as 60,000 CR, while its grade 3 is 1,500,000 and every other weapon's grade 2 is 200,000 to 700,000, which looks like a dropped digit. Manticore Tormentor grade 2 is 500,000 against grade 3 750,000, breaking the ratio every other weapon follows. The shopping list totals will be off by that much. Also, View.svelte:277 shows only how many values are uncertain and never which ones, so the RDX mislink on Headshot Damage (Kinematic) cannot be seen per pick. Recommendation: add these values to astromech-h9ch's checklist. Optionally, list data_uncertain behind a disclosure on the page.
