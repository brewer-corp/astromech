R6 [low] Horizons-era values are coarse: no Horizons species has a journal symbol in the catalogue, so catalog::sample_value values every anemone, brain tree and sinuous tuber at its genus's minimum (Croceum Anemone 3,399,800 counted as 1,499,900; the five brain trees the wiki prices at 3,565,100 counted as 1,593,700). The species symbols are public journal facts (;, ;, ;, ...), and ScanOrganic also carries Species_Localised. Note the wiki itself italicises the 3,565,100 brain tree values and the 3,626,400 amphora value, so these are uncertain either way. Also Elite Observatory (Botanist.cs) maps $Codex_Ent_Seed_Name; as a genus symbol for brain trees, which catalog::genus does not know. Fix later: add Horizons species symbols and the Seed alias; until then the min is a defensible lower bound and the doc says so.
