R1 [high] Licence/provenance: the growing conditions in data/catalog/exobiology.json are in substance EDMC-BioScan's (GPL-2.0) rulesets. Compared with Silarn/EDMC-BioScan src/bio_scan/bio_data/rulesets/*.py at fc6b85c (reference only, nothing copied): all 224 rulesets for the 102 Odyssey species are identical (same ruleset count and split per species, same atmosphere and body-type lists, all 955 numeric bounds equal, e.g. Aleoida Arcus min_pressure 0.0161, max_gravity 0.276, Gravis max_temp 197 where the wiki says 195), and the 'unmodeled' tags reproduce BioScan's own extra keys (regions, atmosphere_component x11, nebula, distance). ExplorerLite's README says the data was 'independently transcribed from public sources, cross-checked against EDMC-BioScan (GPLv2)', but independent transcription cannot reproduce 955 of 955 values and BioScan's rule structure; ExplorerLite's species_rulesets.json was added in one commit (b673da6 'Code cleanup'). The brief says no BioScan data may be used, and NOTICE.md says 'none of their code or data is used', which is not accurate in substance. Whether threshold facts are protectable is a legal judgement, but the project rule is violated as written. Fix: do not merge the conditions until Sumeet decides (file a decision record, needs-human): accept the risk with an accurate NOTICE (naming the BioScan lineage), or replace the conditions with ones derived independently (e.g. empirical ranges from Spansh/EDDN bodies with reported species, which the fixture pipeline already fetches, plus the wiki's coarser per-genus conditions).
