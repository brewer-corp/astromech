[low] NOTICE.md:74-78 (Engineer unlocks): data/catalog/engineer-unlocks.json is generated from the Fandom wiki's Engineer Summary tables. The invite, unlock, reputation and referral phrases are copied word for word after markup is stripped (I re-ran scripts/gen-engineer-unlocks.py on the live wikitext and got identical output). The credit names the page and says 'CC BY-SA', but it doesn't link the licence (CC BY-SA 3.0), say the text was adapted, or say the file is offered under CC BY-SA in an otherwise MIT repository, all of which the licence asks for. The phrases are short and mostly factual, and the Powerplay entry (NOTICE.md:109-112) has the same gap, so the risk is low. Fix both entries together: add the licence URL and a sentence saying these data files are adapted wiki text under CC BY-SA 3.0.
