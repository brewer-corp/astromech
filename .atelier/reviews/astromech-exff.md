The trade merge (04b6f78) and the filters cleanup (63e0399) are approved. Both features keep their tested behaviour. A real-data probe of the full grammar (52 phrases), run on HEAD and on main 2412d3b, found every trade phrase that worked on main still gives the same command, slot and decision on HEAD: buy gold, where can I sell painite, LTDs, sell my cargo, pain night (asks). Station, module and ship phrases (buy a Krait, buy a 5A FSD, nearest interstellar factors, buy a multi cannon) now execute, where main sent them to commodity guesses. Ship commands and status.cargo are unchanged. The margin rule only skips near-match slot rivals when the best slot was heard exactly; two exact slots still ask. The new filters_for matches main's trade::filters_for because for_ship is idempotent after filters::load. search_origin is correct for galaxy_nearby, stations_nearby, trade_search and trade_cargo, and all are in generate_handler. The trade pages now resolve the ship pad on every search when pad_set is false; before, it was resolved when the page loaded. That is consistent and fixes a stale pad after a ship change. Alias learning follows main's no-slot rule, which also covers the station lookups. Only one test was lost, and its assertion is covered elsewhere (finding astromech-ys5z, no_action). Checks run: fmt, clippy -D warnings, cargo test --workspace, check-deps, cargo metadata --locked (app and root), pnpm install/check/test (85 passed). All passed.
